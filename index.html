About this calculator

This free online calculator handles everyday arithmetic: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentages. It runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is sent anywhere, and it works the same on a phone, tablet or computer.

How to use it

Tap or click the keys, or type on your keyboard. Use the digits and . for numbers, + - * / for operations, Enter for the result, Backspace to delete a digit and Esc to clear everything. Press equals repeatedly to repeat the last operation.

How do I calculate a percentage?

Type a number and press % to turn it into a percentage of one, so 50 becomes 0.5. When adding or subtracting, the percent key works on the first number: 200 + 10 % = 220, because 10% of 200 is 20.

Does it follow the order of operations?

Like a basic pocket calculator, it evaluates each operation as you enter it, from left to right. So 2 + 3 × 4 gives 20. To get 14, enter 3 × 4 first and then add 2.

Is it accurate with decimals?

Yes. Results are rounded to remove the tiny floating-point errors common in computers, so 0.1 + 0.2 correctly shows 0.3. Dividing by zero shows a clear message instead of a wrong answer.
